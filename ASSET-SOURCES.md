# Product photography

The catalogue now uses only the 26 photographs supplied by the owner on 15 October 2026. The previous stock photographs have been removed. Repeat photographs are grouped into galleries for the same product. These images do not establish farm ownership, provenance, stock levels, or ongoing availability.

Original source files remain untouched in the two supplied Downloads folders. Website copies have been resized and converted to WebP. The WhatsApp status interface and background people in the alternate capsicum image are cropped out. The hero and bulk enquiry images reuse supplied capsicum photographs.
